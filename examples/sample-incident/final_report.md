INCIDENT RESOLVED — 100x revenue inflation from cloudpay_v2 minor-unit amounts.

SYMPTOM
marts.exec_revenue_metric.revenue read 2,737,323.50 for kpi_day 2026-08-09 vs an expected 29,349.39 (anomaly ratio 93.3x). Baseline comparison showed a clean step change with onset exactly 2026-08-07: revenue_ratio 100.0 / 100.0 / 99.99 on 08-07/08/09 while 07-31..08-06 sat at 1.0 [ev_99f62985f4, ev_ac73812134]. Not a volume event — order_count matched baseline exactly on all three days (334/349/327); the entire distortion was per-order, with aov_median_usd inflated 100x (6483.50 vs 64.84, 5972.00 vs 59.72, 6213.00 vs 62.13) [ev_99f62985f4].

LINEAGE PATH
DataHub column-level lineage yielded a single contributing path: raw.raw_orders.amount → staging.stg_orders.amount → marts.fct_revenue.revenue_usd → marts.exec_revenue_metric.revenue, with stg_fx_rates.usd_rate as the only other branch [ev_8659030b83]. The FX branch was eliminated: usd_rate stayed stable and in-range across the onset window (CAD 0.7179–0.7198, EUR 1.0705–1.0798, GBP 1.2696–1.2746) — no 100x scaling [ev_66422a90c1]. The transforms were eliminated as the origin: stg_orders.sql only CAST amount to DOUBLE and filtered cancelled/refunded [ev_07ca87974f, ev_48dcf900b0]; fct_revenue.sql only aggregated amount * usd_rate [ev_143dc8d308]. The 100x therefore already existed in the raw feed.

ROOT CAUSE (origin isolated by cohort)
Profiling raw.raw_orders.amount segmented by payment_processor showed a complete provider cutover on 2026-08-07: through 08-06 all orders came from legacy_pos and shopgate (mean ~79–86, median ~60–70); from 08-07 onward 100% came from a new processor, cloudpay_v2, with mean 7873–8239, median 5955–6374, max 55262 [ev_46ed4fc52d]. Every cloudpay_v2 row is integer-valued — 1062/1062, first_day 2026-08-07, last_day 2026-08-09 — while legacy rows carry two decimals [ev_2a0e1a6df2, ev_1384d1d5d5]: the signature of integer minor units. DataHub documents the field as "Order amount in MAJOR currency units as a decimal (e.g. 49.99 = $49.99). Contract v1.3: two decimal places" and documents cloudpay_v2 as a provider being rolled out in early August 2026 — the rollout is expected, emitting cents is the defect [ev_b3a432a9b9]. Necessary and sufficient: rescaling only the cloudpay_v2 cohort by 1/100 reproduced the committed baseline to the cent [ev_082bf199fc]; pre-repair the invariant suite failed 7/32, all downstream expressions of this one defect [ev_c422535786].

REPAIR
pipeline/transforms/stg_orders.sql — normalized the minor-unit cohort to major units at the ingestion-contract boundary the data team owns, via CASE WHEN payment_processor = 'cloudpay_v2' THEN CAST(amount AS DOUBLE)/100.0 ELSE CAST(amount AS DOUBLE) END, with a comment documenting the contract and incident. Scoped strictly to the defective provider so legacy_pos/shopgate history is untouched; no rows dropped, filtered, or hidden (14 insertions, 1 deletion).

VERIFICATION
Warehouse rebuilt: full invariant suite 32/32 passed (was 25/32), KPI anomaly ratio 0.9327 vs the prior 93.3x. Post-repair baseline comparison shows revenue_ratio and aov_ratio of exactly 1.0 for 08-07/08/09 (26,557.18 / 27,383.61 / 27,373.23 revenue; 64.84 / 59.72 / 62.13 median AOV) with order_count unchanged, and healthy history 08-04..08-06 also still at ratio 1.0 [ev_20e5f9806d]. Shipped on branch blackbox/fix-inc_ee7f7471fb @ 018ef58113; DataHub incident marked RESOLVED (FIXED).

FOLLOW-UP FOR OWNERS: the durable fix belongs with the cloudpay_v2 integration — have the provider emit major units per contract v1.3, and add an ingestion assertion on amount decimal precision per payment_processor so a future provider cutover fails loudly instead of silently multiplying revenue.
